\begin{abstract}
\noindent
When LLM agents commit by best-of-$n$ (or related) selection under an imperfect critic, selection concentrates Goodhart errors: high selecting-critic score, verified task failure.
We call these events \emph{critic blind spots}.
\textbf{Primary unit of analysis:} one completed \emph{trajectory} $\tau$; gold $g(\tau)\in\{0,1\}$ is trajectory-level task success (e.g., SWE-bench tests), never a claim that every intermediate step was invalid.
\textbf{Open cell:} among critic-selected trajectories, does audit-panel uncertainty $u$ improve held-out discrimination of $\gzero$ \emph{beyond} the selecting-critic score $c$, under a \emph{disjoint} selection-critic vs audit-panel design?
We propose \textbf{SilentFail-Bench}: a reproducible offline protocol with a frozen logistic combining model, repository-level train/test splits, a 200-trajectory pilot that sets power and go/no-go, and a quality-controlled diversity analysis (association, not causal mediation by default).
A null is first-class and safety-relevant.
\end{abstract}

\section{Motivation and Main Claim}

\paragraph{Why it matters.}
Agent failures compound mid-task~\cite{agentforesight,detectrepair,falsesuccess}.
Many scaffolds select among candidates using a critic score~\cite{lightman,mathshepherd,agentprm}.
Under Goodhart and the winner's curse in best-of-$n$~\cite{gao2023,inferencetimehacking,caution,minin}, optimizing proxy $c$ enriches the \emph{selected} set for critic mistakes.
Repair needs a trigger~\cite{reflexion}.
Prior UQ either changes \emph{selection} (pessimistic BoN)~\cite{coste,caution,urm}, filters unconditionally, or uses agent verbal confidence (weak early)~\cite{laststep}.
None answer: given the trajectory the agent already committed to, does a separate audit signal $u$ still help beyond $c$?

\paragraph{Main claim (selection-bias / Goodhart).}
Conditional on critic selection, disagreement among a \emph{disjoint} audit panel is associated with residual information about trajectory failure beyond $c$.
Lower pairwise error correlation across quality-matched panels is \emph{associated} with larger residual detection (mechanism analysis; not a causal mediation claim unless diversity is randomized under fixed quality).
Falsifiable null: even diverse panels share blind spots under selection.

\paragraph{What we do not claim.}
We do not invent ``silent failures''~\cite{falsesuccess}; do not propose a new auditor~\cite{agentforesight}; do not claim first use of RM uncertainty~\cite{urm,coste,caution}.
Novelty is the \textbf{selection-conditional, beyond-score, held-out estimand on agent trajectories with disjoint audit panels + pilot-powered SilentFail-Bench}.

\section{Notation (locked)}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=1pt]
  \item $\tau$: one completed agent trajectory (primary observation).
  \item $\mathrm{sel}$: the selection event that committed $\tau$ (best-of-$n$ over candidate trajectories, or top-$K$ integer retention from a fixed pool; defined per experiment).
  \item $c$: score from the \emph{selection critic} $c_{\mathrm{sel}}$ on the committed $\tau$ (higher $c$ $=$ predicted higher quality / success).
  \item $u$: disagreement statistic from an \emph{audit panel} (higher $u$ $=$ more disagreement).
  \item $g(\tau)\in\{0,1\}$: trajectory-level gold; $g{=}1$ iff verified task success (unit tests / env reward). Positive class for AUROC: $\gzero$ (failure).
  \item $n$: candidate-pool size for best-of-$n$ (select top-1).
  \item $K$: integer number retained under top-$K$ filtering (not a percent of small $n$).
  \item $m$: audit-panel size.
  \item $b$: selective-verification budget (fraction of selected trajectories flagged for review).
\end{itemize}

\noindent\textbf{Critical gap.}
To our knowledge, no public evaluation jointly reports:
(i)~held-out $\DeltaAUROC$ of audit uncertainty beyond selecting-critic score,
(ii)~conditional on critic selection of LLM-agent trajectories,
(iii)~trajectory-verified gold,
(iv)~a disjoint selection-critic vs audit-panel design, and
(v)~quality-controlled diversity comparisons.
Closest work uses uncertainty to \emph{change} BoN selection~\cite{caution,urm,minin}, not to detect residual failure \emph{after} selection.

\section{Gold Label and Unit of Analysis}

\paragraph{Primary endpoint.}
One observation $=$ one selected trajectory $\tau$.
$g(\tau)$ is trajectory-level verified success/failure.
A failed final test suite means the \emph{trajectory} failed; it does \textbf{not} prove that every intermediate action was invalid.
A \emph{critic blind spot} is a selected trajectory with high $c$ and $\gzero$.

\paragraph{How many observations.}
Each trajectory contributes \textbf{exactly one} primary observation $(c,u,g,\mathrm{controls})$.
Train/test splits and bootstrap CIs are clustered by trajectory (and by repository when splitting).

\paragraph{Secondary (optional, clearly approximate).}
Step-level claims use Math-Shepherd-style Monte-Carlo rollouts from a state (``still on a succeeding path'')~\cite{mathshepherd}, reported with rollout-noise sensitivity and a hierarchical model clustered by trajectory.
Step labels are never treated as exact oracles.

\section{Selection Critic vs Audit Panel (Leakage Control)}

\paragraph{Disjoint (primary).}
Selection critic $c_{\mathrm{sel}}$ chooses $\tau$.
A separate frozen audit panel $\{c_1,\ldots,c_m\}$ with $c_{\mathrm{sel}}\notin\{c_1,\ldots,c_m\}$ produces $u$.
This is the clean beyond-score test.

\paragraph{Shared (ablation only).}
$c_{\mathrm{sel}}$ is included in the panel (deployment-realistic, coupled).
Reported separately; not the primary confirmatory contrast.

\paragraph{Definitions of $c$ and $u$ (primary).}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=1pt]
  \item $c := c_{\mathrm{sel}}(\tau)$, the selecting critic's score on the committed trajectory.
  \item Rank-normalize each audit critic's scores using \emph{training-split} rank parameters only (frozen at test time).
  \item Primary $u :=$ sample standard deviation of the $m$ rank-normalized audit scores on $\tau$.
  \item Exploratory $u$: range; mean pairwise rank disagreement (labeled exploratory).
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Combining model (frozen, named).}
Let $x_{\mathrm{ctrl}}=(\mathrm{length},\,\mathrm{step\text{-}count},\,\mathrm{mean\ agent\ entropy})$.
Fit on training repositories only:
\begin{align*}
M_{c}:&\quad \Pr(\gzero)=\sigma(\beta_0+\beta_c c+\beta_{\mathrm{ctrl}}^\top x_{\mathrm{ctrl}}),\\
M_{u}:&\quad \Pr(\gzero)=\sigma(\beta_0+\beta_u u+\beta_{\mathrm{ctrl}}^\top x_{\mathrm{ctrl}}),\\
M_{c{+}u}:&\quad \Pr(\gzero)=\sigma(\beta_0+\beta_c c+\beta_u u+\beta_{\mathrm{ctrl}}^\top x_{\mathrm{ctrl}}).
\end{align*}
Held-out AUROCs use predicted failure probabilities.
\[
\DeltaAUROC := \mathrm{AUROC}_{\mathrm{held\text{-}out}}(M_{c{+}u})-\mathrm{AUROC}_{\mathrm{held\text{-}out}}(M_{c}).
\]
Also report $M_{u}$ alone, AUPRC (class imbalance), LR test for $\beta_u$ in $M_{c{+}u}$, and trajectory-/repo-clustered bootstrap 95\% CIs for $\DeltaAUROC$.

\paragraph{Confirmatory vs exploratory.}
\textbf{Confirmatory:} disjoint-panel $\DeltaAUROC$ at the anchor (diverse vs quality-matched seed panel).
\textbf{Exploratory:} shared-panel ablation; alternate $u$ stats; secondary step labels; full pressure$\times$diversity grid; E4--E7.
Multiple exploratory slices use BH-FDR or are clearly marked non-confirmatory.

\section{Pilot, Power, and Compute}

\paragraph{200-trajectory pilot (Stage 0; before full matrix).}
Anchor configuration only. Report: \#trajectories; \#selection decisions logged; selected-set failure count and rate with CI; $c$ and $u$ distributions; $c$--$u$ correlation (range restriction); exclusions.

\paragraph{Go/no-go (numeric thresholds set from pilot, not hopes).}
Continue to Stage 1 only if:
(i)~enough $\gzero$ cases in the selected set for useful precision on $\DeltaAUROC$ (pilot simulation sets the count), and
(ii)~$M_{c}$ alone does not already saturate (held-out AUROC headroom for $u$).
Planning assumption only (not a result): failure rates on hard agent benches are often non-trivial; the pilot replaces any 10--20\% target.

\paragraph{Power.}
Simulation-based power using observed pilot base rate, within-repo clustering, and the train/test split.
Predeclare minimum detectable $\DeltaAUROC$ worth pursuing (set after pilot; planning placeholder $\DeltaAUROC\geq 0.03$ pending pilot).
Report required trajectories/failures for 80\% and 90\% power.

\section{Selection-Pressure Specification}

Best-of-$n$ and top-$K$ are \textbf{not} interchangeable.

\paragraph{Best-of-$n$ (primary pressure axis).}
Candidate pool size $=n$; retain exactly 1 (argmax of $c_{\mathrm{sel}}$).
Sweep $n\in\{4,8,16,32\}$.
Anchor: $n{=}16$ (not paired with a percent).

\paragraph{Top-$K$ filtering (separate experiment).}
Fix pool size $M{=}64$ candidates; retain integer $K\in\{1,2,4,8\}$.
Never write ``$k{=}5\%$ of $n{=}16$'' (undefined / sub-integer).

For every pressure cell, the paper will list $(M\text{ or }n,\ \text{retained},\ \text{rule})$.

\section{Diversity vs Critic Quality}

Diversity interventions (seed / data / base / public-zoo) are separate conditions, not one assumed ordinal scale unless the pilot supports ordering.

Before comparing panels on $\DeltaAUROC$:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=1pt]
  \item Match or regress on per-critic quality (RewardBench-style accuracy/calibration and/or held-out ranking accuracy on train repos).
  \item Report per-critic accuracy alongside pairwise error correlation on gold.
  \item Primary diversity contrast: quality-matched seed panel vs quality-matched base-diverse panel at fixed $m$.
\end{itemize}
Default claim language: error correlation is \emph{associated} with residual detection.
``Mediation'' reserved only if treatment (diversity) is randomized under fixed quality with stated assumptions; otherwise ``mechanism analysis.''

\section{Preregistered Anchor}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=1pt]
  \item \textbf{Tasks:} SWE-bench Verified; split by repository (70/30, seed 20260922).
  \item \textbf{Agent scaffold:} mini-swe-agent (commit pinned in \texttt{silentfail\_bench/configs/anchor.yaml} before pilot).
  \item \textbf{Score rule:} $c$ $=$ selection-critic scalar on the \emph{full} committed trajectory text (messages + patch); BoN ranks full trajectories; no per-step mean.
  \item \textbf{Selection critic:} Skywork-Reward-V2-Qwen3-8B (HF id in anchor config); $c=c_{\mathrm{sel}}(\tau)$.
  \item \textbf{Audit panel (disjoint):} $m{=}4$ public RMs listed in anchor config; none equal to $c_{\mathrm{sel}}$.
  \item \textbf{Selection rule:} best-of-$n$ with $n{=}16$, retain top-1; $T{=}0.7$; max steps 50; timeout 1800s.
  \item \textbf{$u$:} std.\ of train-fit rank-normalized audit scores.
  \item \textbf{Combining model:} logistic $M_{c}$, $M_{c{+}u}$; controls $=$ length, step-count, mean entropy.
  \item \textbf{Compute:} vLLM OpenAI-compatible server and/or hosted API; logistic fit on CPU.
  \item \textbf{Primary endpoint:} held-out $\DeltaAUROC$ (failure positive).
  \item \textbf{Primary contrast:} quality-matched diverse vs seed audit panel (disjoint).
  \item \textbf{CI:} repository-clustered bootstrap 95\%.
  \item \textbf{Exclusions:} empty candidate pools; critic NaN; timeout; max steps exceeded.
  \item \textbf{Stage-0 code:} \texttt{silentfail\_bench/} (schema, mock/vLLM runner, pilot report, power helpers).
\end{itemize}

\noindent\textbf{Controls vs baselines.}
Length, step-count, and entropy enter $M_c$/$M_{c{+}u}$ as covariates so residual $u$ is not a proxy for verbosity or horizon.
The same features also appear as operational baselines in E7.

\paragraph{Robustness.}
Repo holdouts; AUPRC; calibration (ECE); sensitivity to $u$ statistic, normalization, panel composition; report selected-set range restriction ($c$--$u$ corr.) as a result.

\section{Ideal Results (pilot-informed)}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=1pt]
  \item Pilot yields a measurable selected-set failure rate with CI (replaces any fixed 10--20\% hope).
  \item Disjoint-panel held-out $\DeltaAUROC > 0$ at anchor with clustered CI excluding 0 (or a clean null).
  \item Quality-matched lower error correlation is associated with larger $\DeltaAUROC$.
  \item Exploratory: flatter $\DeltaAUROC$ decay in $\log n$ for diverse vs seed panels.
  \item E7: at fixed $b$, $M_{c{+}u}$ catches more failures per review than $M_c$ alone.
  \item Alt.\ ideal: diverse-panel null $\Rightarrow$ negative-result safety paper~\cite{eisenstein}.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Limitations and Staging}

Trajectory $g$ does not credit-assign steps; secondary step labels are noisy.
Offline E7 $\neq$ adaptive recovery.
Critic scale mismatch mitigated by train-only rank-norm, not eliminated.
Logging cost may force confirmatory-only scope.
Range restriction may erase headroom (informative).
Scaffold/critic hashes must be frozen before Stage 0.

\textbf{Stage 0:} 200-traj.\ pilot + go/no-go + power + cost.
\textbf{Stage 1:} confirmatory anchor only.
\textbf{Stage 2:} exploratory matrix as affordable.
